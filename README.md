# CHARGALI LEGACY — Investment & Mountain Hospitality Hub
**Location:** Chargali Village, Pshavi (Dusheti Municipality, Mtskheta-Mtianeti, Georgia)  
**Official Website:** [https://chargalilegacy.github.io/](https://chargalilegacy.github.io/)

---

### Overview
**Chargali Legacy** is an independent private land and mountain hospitality development initiative located at ~1,200 m above sea level, approximately 80 km from Tbilisi. The project integrates eco-tourism, boutique mountain accommodation, and long-term regional development in one of Georgia's most iconic highland destinations.

### Phase I — Cottage Plot Investment
We offer a limited number of **~200 m² land plots** in Chargali village for private investors and hospitality partners:
* Individual acquisition and legal ownership;
* Turnkey ethno-cottage construction or self-development aligned with the unified architectural concept;
* Flexible partnership terms negotiated individually.
